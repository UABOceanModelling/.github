# Welcome to Our Ocean Modelling Lab 🌊

The Myers Ocean Modelling group carries out research on the ocean processes and the ocean's role in the climate system. Much of this work is carried out with numerical models, especially the NEMO model. Multiple configurations are used, varying from 1/4 degree for testing and long integrations, up to 1/60 degree to look at the role of the mesoscale and sub-mesoscale in driving key oceanic processes. We also use the AGRIF nesting tools to allow for variable spatial resolution, focussing on key study areas like the Labrador Sea.
